# Euphoria — MITE Cultural Club app

The official app of **Euphoria, the Cultural Club of MITE**: club sessions and reminders, announcements from your club leaders, event registration, the gallery and the hall of fame.

Clubs: Razzle · Chords · Captcha · Pixel · Speakers League · Mask · Mystique · Palette

## Download

**Android:** open the [latest release](https://github.com/VidwathN-Karanth/Euphoria-App-Releases/releases/latest) and download the `.apk` file.

**iPhone:** the App Store version is on its way. Until then, open **https://euphoria-mite.vercel.app** in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

## Installing on Android

1. Download the `.apk` from the latest release on your phone.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to **install unknown apps**.
3. Open Euphoria and sign in with your email — you'll get a 6-digit code.

Updating: install the newer `.apk` over the old one; you stay signed in.
